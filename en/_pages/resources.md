## Why These Resources Are Important

### For Students and Researchers
- **Visibility** – Make your research discoverable
- **Networking** – Connect with other researchers
- **Citations** – Track impact of your work
- **Open Access** – Share your publications freely

### For Academic Career
- **ORCID** is increasingly required by journals
- **Google Scholar** profiles appear in search results
- **ResearchGate** allows collaboration
- **GitHub** showcases your code and projects
